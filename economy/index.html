Trust signal

This page avoids stale metrics in favor of durable clarity.

Product confidence drops when old dashboards and outdated numbers dominate the story. The redesign explains the economy through structure, not fragile statistics.

Positioning

The economy page should reassure first, then intrigue.

A first-touch economy page does not need to prove everything at once. It needs to show visitors that there is a coherent system behind the marketplace and that participation has meaningful texture.

That is why the redesign emphasizes structure, access, and player intent instead of drowning the page in dashboard fragments or repeated CTAs.

Page discipline

One page, one job.

Here the job is simple: make the economy feel coherent, premium, and worth engaging with.

Questions

What needs to be explicit.

Why remove detailed live stats from the hero?
Because stale proof erodes trust. Unless metrics can stay current and contextual, the page is stronger without them.

How does this stay premium instead of reading like financial software?
The visual system keeps the economy in the same cinematic world as the rest of the site, while the copy stays direct and high-trust.
