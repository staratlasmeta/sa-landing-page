Why not put a general form in the hero?
Because undifferentiated contact forms create more uncertainty. Routing by need is faster for the visitor and easier to maintain.
How should external support destinations be handled?
Always label them clearly so visitors know when they are leaving the main marketing shell.
What if support volume grows?
This template is designed to absorb more specialized paths over time while keeping the first decision simple.
