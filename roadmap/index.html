Roadmap rules

What this page should never do again.

No clutter

Do not compete with the hero

The roadmap should clarify state, not bury it under repeated CTAs or utility chrome.

No confusion

Use one consistent product-state vocabulary

If the product is playable in some surfaces and expanding in others, say that plainly and consistently everywhere.

No stale theater

Remove brittle urgency signals

Countdowns, old campaign artifacts, and unclear launch labels make the product feel less premium.

Questions

How to keep roadmap messaging trustworthy.

Why use now, next, and later instead of dated quarters everywhere?
Because this structure stays useful when schedules shift and avoids the trust erosion that comes from stale date promises.
What should be mirrored on the homepage?
Only the high-level status summary. The detailed explanation lives here so the homepage stays calm and focused.
